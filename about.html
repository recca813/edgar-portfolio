About Edgar

Agentic AI & Workflow Automation Engineer, Dubai, UAE

I build things that keep working without me watching them. A trading bot that reacts while I'm asleep. A workflow that finds job listings every morning before I wake up. A voice assistant that answers questions using my own notes, no internet needed. If I still have to babysit it, it's not done.

I like building the real thing fast, then fixing what breaks — instead of planning for weeks first. Most of my projects started rough and got better once I saw what actually went wrong. I'd rather ship something useful with a few rough edges than sit on a perfect plan that's never been tested.

Outside of client and personal projects, I spend a lot of time on trading systems — Pine Script indicators, MT5 EAs, and the automation that connects them to Discord and a trade journal — and on running a fully local AI voice pipeline (llama.cpp, RAG over my own Markdown vault, local TTS) mostly because I wanted to see how far "no cloud dependency" could go. Both are the kind of side projects that turn into real infrastructure because I actually use them daily.
