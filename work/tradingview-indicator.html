Multi-Timeframe TradingView Indicator

One chart, market structure from multiple timeframes, and setups that invalidate themselves the moment price proves them wrong.

Pine Script v6 TradingView

01 / Problem

Reading market structure across multiple timeframes normally means flipping between charts and holding the higher-timeframe context in your head while watching the lower one — slow, and easy to get wrong under a moving market.

02 / Approach

Surface the higher-timeframe structure directly on the chart being traded, on a live dashboard, instead of requiring a second chart. Early versions of the indicator suffered from repainting — structure that looked valid in the past but wouldn't have triggered in real time — so fixing that came before adding new features.

03 / What Was Built

A Pine Script v6 indicator that tracks market structure across multiple timeframes and renders it as a live on-chart dashboard, so higher-timeframe context is visible without switching charts. Repaint issues in the structure detection were identified and resolved so what the indicator shows matches what would have actually triggered in real time. Invalidation logic was added on top: a setup automatically voids itself once price breaches a specific wick level, instead of staying marked valid past the point it no longer is.

04 / Outcome

A single-chart view of multi-timeframe structure that's accurate in real time, not just in hindsight, with setups that self-invalidate instead of needing to be manually tracked and dismissed.
